Overview

StepWin is a step-based wellness and rewards app. It may include features such as daily step tracking, calories and distance estimates, hydration tasks, heart-rate related flows, workout courses, check-ins, lucky spin activities, coin balances, redemption, and withdrawal requests.

This Privacy Policy applies to information we collect when you use those features, contact support, participate in reward or redemption activities, or otherwise interact with StepWin services.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • contact details you provide when you contact support, such as your email address;
  • account details you choose to submit, such as nickname, profile information, or login credentials if account features are available;
  • reward and withdrawal information you submit, such as the payout account details needed to process a withdrawal request;
  • communications, feedback, screenshots, or other information you send to us.

Fitness, activity, and task information

  • step counts, estimated calories, walking distance, exercise progress, and similar movement-related data shown in the app;
  • hydration task completion, workout participation, heart-rate related entries or measurement results, and other wellness-task activity if you choose to use those features;
  • daily check-in records, lucky spin participation, coin earnings, reward claims, redemption history, payout status, and withdrawal request status.

Automatically collected information

  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language, app version, time zone, and network status;
  • log, diagnostics, and performance information, such as crash reports, error logs, and basic usage events;
  • security and anti-fraud signals reasonably necessary to protect step rewards, redemption activity, and withdrawals.

Information from third parties or integrations

If StepWin integrates with device motion systems, health or fitness platforms, analytics providers, advertising or attribution partners, payout channels, or app store services, we may receive the information necessary for those integrations to work. The information received depends on your device settings, permissions, and the services you use.

2. Permissions and Device Access

Depending on the features you use, StepWin may request access to device or system permissions such as motion or fitness data for step tracking, notifications for reminders or reward status, and photos or files if you choose to send materials to support.

You can usually manage permissions in your device settings. If you deny certain permissions, some StepWin features may not function properly.

3. How We Use Information

  • provide, maintain, and improve StepWin and its fitness, reward, and support features;
  • track steps and task progress, calculate coins or other earned rewards, and display balances, check-ins, spins, and redemption status;
  • review, process, and protect withdrawals and other reward transactions;
  • detect abuse, cheating, simulated activity, duplicate-account abuse, fraud, unauthorized activity, or security incidents;
  • troubleshoot performance issues and improve app stability and user experience;
  • respond to support requests, feedback, and account-related communications;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in the following situations:

  • with service providers that help us operate the app, customer support, analytics, hosting, fraud prevention, or withdrawal processing;
  • with payout or payment partners when required to review or complete a withdrawal request;
  • with professional advisers, regulators, courts, law enforcement, or other third parties when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security;
  • in connection with a merger, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, subject to applicable confidentiality and legal requirements;
  • with your consent or at your direction.

5. Data Retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide StepWin, operate rewards and withdrawals, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, comply with legal obligations, and protect the security and integrity of the service.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected. We may retain limited records after account closure where required for security, fraud prevention, accounting, or legal compliance.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

To submit a privacy request, contact us at stepsupport.feedback@gmail.com. We may ask for reasonable verification before completing your request.

You may also manage certain information through your device settings, app settings, or by choosing not to use optional features. If you request account deletion or uninstall the app, some reward, balance, or withdrawal-related information may no longer remain available to you except where we must retain limited records for security, accounting, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. However, no security measure is perfect, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

StepWin is not intended for children under 13, or under the minimum age required by applicable law in your jurisdiction. If we learn that we collected personal information from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

Health-Related Features

Some StepWin features may relate to wellness activity, such as step tracking, hydration tasks, workout participation, or heart-rate related flows. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, these features are provided for general wellness or informational purposes only and are not medical services or medical advice.

9. International Processing

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than the country in which you live. Where required, we will take reasonable steps intended to protect transferred information in accordance with applicable law.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of StepWin after an update becomes effective means the updated Privacy Policy will apply to your use of the service.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions, privacy requests, or support issues related to this Privacy Policy, contact us at stepsupport.feedback@gmail.com.
